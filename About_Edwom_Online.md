Edwom Online - Your Convenient Grocery Delivery Solution
Business Overview
Edwom Online is a modern e-commerce grocery platform designed to revolutionize how homes and individuals shop for their daily essentials. Our mission is to provide customers with the convenience of shopping from the comfort of their homes while ensuring fresh, quality products are delivered directly to their doorstep.
Key Value Propositions:
Convenient home delivery service
Fresh, quality groceries and household items
Curated meal packages with cooking guidance
User-friendly browsing and shopping experience
Flexible shopping options (cart-based or list-based ordering)
Target Audience
Busy professionals seeking convenience
Families looking to streamline grocery shopping
Individuals who prefer home delivery
Cooking enthusiasts interested in meal packages
Anyone seeking a reliable grocery delivery service

🛒 User Features – Edwom Online Grocery Shop
Landing Page
Welcome message with brand story, benefits of using Edwom Online.
Featured categories, trending packages, and seasonal highlights.
Call-to-action buttons for sign-up, login, and browsing.
Authentication
Sign up and log in via email/password or Google.
Password reset and email verification.
Optional guest browsing with limited features.
Cart Functionality
Add/remove items with quantity control.
Add to cart without login; prompt login/signup at checkout.
Persistent cart (items saved across sessions if logged in).
Browsing & Search
Browse by categories (e.g., Fruits, Vegetables, Meats, Spices).
Search bar with autocomplete and filters (price, availability, etc.).
View seasonal items and price hikes clearly marked.
Packages & Cooking Videos
View curated packages (e.g., “Jollof Rice Pack”) with all ingredients.
Embedded YouTube cooking videos for each package.
Option to add entire package to cart or select individual items.
Checkout Process
View all cart items with total cost.
Checkout with location input (GPS pin or typed address).
Payment handled externally (e.g., mobile money, bank transfer).
Custom Shopping List
Create a list of items without adding to cart.
Option to save, share, or convert to cart later.
Seasonal Pricing
Items marked as “Out of Season” with price hike indicator.
Tooltip or info icon explaining the reason for price change.
User Profile
View order history, saved lists, and preferred location.
Edit profile details and delivery location
Notifications
Email or in-app alerts for order status, promotions, and seasonal updates.

🛠️ Admin Features – Edwom Online Dashboard
Item Management
Add, edit, delete items with details: name, category, quantity, unit, discount, seasonal status.
Bulk upload via CSV or Excel.
Configuration Settings
Manage categories, measuring units, discount rules, and seasonal flags.
Set featured items and packages.
Package Management
Create/edit/delete packages with linked items.
Add YouTube video links and descriptions.
Order Management
View all orders with filters (date, status, user).
Update order status (e.g., pending, processing, delivered).
Assign an order to self. (So we know who is working on what order)
Admin Authentication
Secure login with role-based access (e.g., Super Admin, Admin).
Activity logs for admin actions.
To-Do List
Internal task manager for admins (e.g., restock alerts, promotions).
Assign tasks and set deadlines.
Analytics Dashboard
Sales trends, popular items/packages, user activity.
Export reports (PDF, Excel).
Admin Management
Add/edit/delete admin accounts.
Assign roles and permissions.
User Management
View user list with details (name, email, location, order history).
Option to deactivate or flag suspicious accounts.
Promotions & Announcements
Create banners or pop-ups for seasonal sales, new packages, or updates.
